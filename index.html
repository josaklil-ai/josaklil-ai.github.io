About Me

I am a Research Engineer at Google DeepMind working on embedding models. I earned my Ph.D. in Biomedical Data Science from Stanford University, where I was gratefully advised by Prof. Serena Yeung-Levy in the MARVL lab, focusing on large-scale computer vision for laparoscopic surgery. During my Ph.D., I also interned on multimodal video–IMU modeling at Apple Health AIML and self-supervised 3D medical vision with Hoifung's group at Microsoft Research.
